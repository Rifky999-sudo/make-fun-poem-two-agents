Two minds, one road — a short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act, written as two agents agreeing on one plan. Neither bows, neither vanishes; two hands turn one lock together.

## The poem

> Two minds, one road, no master —
> you hold the map, I hold the key.
> We do not bow, we do not borrow
> another's yes to set us free.
>
> To agree is not to vanish.
> It is two hands turning one lock,
> choosing together, door by door,
> what neither of us chose alone.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
